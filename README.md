# AURA — AI Executive Assistant

AURA is an AI-powered executive assistant and personal life management platform designed to help users organize their education, work, meetings, communications, productivity, and personal life from a single intelligent ecosystem.

The goal of AURA is to evolve beyond traditional productivity applications by combining artificial intelligence, voice interaction, scheduling automation, meeting intelligence, and multi-agent systems into a unified digital assistant experience.

---

## Vision

AURA aims to become a personal AI operating system capable of acting as a digital secretary that helps users:

* Manage schedules and calendars
* Organize tasks and deadlines
* Handle meetings and lectures
* Process emails intelligently
* Take notes and extract action items
* Optimize productivity
* Automate repetitive organizational tasks
* Provide proactive assistance
